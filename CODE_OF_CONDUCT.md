# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PULSE is intended to be a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive open-source project.

We welcome participation regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality, disability, or personal circumstances.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical disagreements.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, and decisions rather than individuals.
- Protect personal and health information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, sharing private health data or credentials, and publishing exposed secrets without responsible disclosure are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, close, or restrict participation in content or interactions that violate this code. Reports should be sent privately to the repository maintainers without including sensitive material.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other spaces operated for PULSE.
